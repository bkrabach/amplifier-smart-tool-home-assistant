# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and perspectives,
give feedback about work rather than people, and help keep discussion useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior
are not welcome. Do not post personal information, raw logs, credentials,
device inventories, household details, or other unsafe home information.

## Moderation

The maintainer, @bkrabach, may remove content or restrict interactions to keep
the project safe and constructive.

## Reporting a concern

For a concern that can be described without personal or sensitive information,
contact the maintainer in this repository's issue tracker. For a sensitive
concern, use a private contact method already available to you; otherwise post
only a request for a private reporting channel, not the details.

Do not use a security report for non-security conduct concerns. GitHub platform
abuse can be reported through GitHub's documented reporting process:

https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies#reporting-violations-of-githubs-acceptable-use-policies
